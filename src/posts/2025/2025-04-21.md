I used to write a blog a long time ago now. It covered a variety of topics, but the one I could religiously churn out was my *This Week in Music* feature. 

You may be surprised to learn it was not in fact weekly, but whenever I could be bothered. 

That equated to 30 posts between 2012 & 2015. 

Given it's Easter weekend, why don't I participate in resurrection myself? 

## Di Melo - Kilariô
When I used to work in an office, I was - to my own surprise - often the first one in, as I wanted to shower having cycled in. I'd chuck on my *Morning Coffee* playlist, which was absolutely filled with <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropic%C3%A1lia" target="_blank">Tropicalia</a> classics. 

I was revisiting the playlist recently and Di Melo's Kilariô came on, the intro is infectiously funky, and the track never lets up from that point.
